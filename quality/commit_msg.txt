E2E 过程中发现一个真实 bug：

- stage[spec] 存的是 boolean body ({all: [...]})，不是 StageSpec 完整
  envelope ({goal, acceptance, ...})
- _synthesize_stage_draft 直接把 body 喂给 StageSpec.from_dict，导致
  acceptance 永远是空，acceptance.checks 走 fallback
  artifact-present:stage:<id>，spec.max_dispatches 等也全是默认
- 修：synthesize 时构造 envelope {goal: stage.title, acceptance: raw_spec}
  再交给 StageSpec.from_dict
